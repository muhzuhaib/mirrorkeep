Privacy

Mirrorkeep privacy policy

Mirrorkeep works entirely on your computer.

  • No data collection. Mirrorkeep does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data, file names, folder paths, or usage statistics.
  • Your files stay yours. Files are copied between folders you choose on your own disks. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is shared. Replaced and deleted files are kept in a safety folder inside the destination until its retention expires.
  • No network access. The app works without any network connection. The only network paths it can touch are destinations you explicitly choose that happen to be on a LAN, a mapped drive or a UNC path, and only Pro accepts those. Licence checks and purchases are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store.
  • No third party services. The app contains no analytics, advertising, telemetry, or crash reporting components.
  • Permissions. The app reads the source folder you choose and writes to the destination and its safety folder, both on your own disks. A schedule you set up is registered with the Windows Task Scheduler so the pair can run while you are signed in. It requests no other access.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version is published on this page and the date below is updated.
